Switching rules

  • Demand above 160 kW switches to Renewable Storage Mode.
  • Hours from 18:00 through 22:00 inclusive also use Renewable Storage Mode.
  • All other hours use Main Grid Mode.

These are software recommendations based on the configured switching rules. They are not commands to physical equipment, and no grid switching takes place.

Alerts

A critical overload is predicted demand of 200 kW or more. Alerts are independent of the grid mode.

About project

GridSense AI is a hackathon project for HackSphere 3.0. A Flask service sends your weather assumptions to a published Zoho Catalyst QuickML regression model, then a rule-based controller labels each predicted hour with a grid operating mode and checks it for critical overload.

Prediction
One QuickML demand prediction per hour for the next 24 hours.
Grid mode
Rule-based recommendation. No physical equipment is controlled.
Weather input
Entered by you and held constant across all 24 hours. No live weather feed is used.
Telemetry
None. Nothing on this page is measured from a real grid.
